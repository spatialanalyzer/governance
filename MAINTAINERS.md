## Role continuity

The Project Founder and Lead Architect retains permanent founder attribution
and remains eligible to serve regardless of employer. Hexagon may replace its
Primary or Backup Focal at its discretion. A replaced focal remains eligible
to continue as a non-focal maintainer if the individual and project wish.

Leaving Boeing, Hexagon, or another employer does not automatically remove an
individual from project participation. Each person remains responsible for
complying with continuing confidentiality, intellectual-property, and other
legal obligations.

## Lead Architect succession

This provisional succession process applies:

1. The serving Lead Architect may nominate a successor.
2. The Hexagon Primary Focal confirms the nominee before the appointment
   becomes effective.
3. If the Lead Architect is unable to nominate a successor, the remaining
   active Founding Maintainers select an interim Lead Architect by consensus.
4. The interim appointment does not alter permanent founder attribution.
5. A permanent selection process should then be documented.

If the remaining Founding Maintainers cannot reach consensus, the deadlock
process in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) applies.

## Access

Maintainers receive only the access needed for their responsibilities.
Administrative access should be held by more than one trusted person once
practical. Credentials, recovery codes, signing keys, and service accounts
must not be shared through public project channels.

Granting or removing platform access does not decide copyright ownership,
founder attribution, corporate authority, or entitlement to project assets.

## Policies not yet established

The project has not yet adopted comprehensive policies for:

- admitting new maintainers;
- inactivity and emeritus status;
- routine maintainer resignation;
- removal for misconduct or sustained non-participation; or
- emergency access removal following compromise or material risk.

Security-critical access may be suspended as a temporary protective measure,
but a lasting role decision must follow the governance process then in effect.
