# Funding, Sponsorship, and Project Assets

## 1. Current funding

The project does not currently have a legal entity, fiscal host, formal
budget, or corporate funding commitment. The Project Founder and Lead
Architect currently pays or administers several project services.

Personal expenditures:

- are voluntary;
- do not create ownership, additional voting power, or priority over project
  decisions;
- are not guaranteed to be reimbursed; and
- should not be represented as charitable or tax-deductible contributions.

## 2. Donations and sponsorships

Maintainers may accept donated services, sponsorships, or payment of project
expenses without prior formal approval.

A maintainer accepting support must:

- avoid promising governance authority, endorsement, exclusivity, or
  intellectual-property rights in return;
- disclose material support and any conditions to the other Founding
  Maintainers;
- publicly disclose material ongoing sponsorship when confidentiality or law
  does not prohibit it;
- identify who legally receives or owns money, accounts, domains, equipment,
  or service credits; and
- avoid representing that an unincorporated project can issue tax receipts or
  enter contracts in its own name.

If offered support requires material contractual commitments, trademark use,
exclusive benefits, or changes to project policy, the applicable governance
decision process must be used before acceptance.

Sponsorship alone grants no decision authority. Any governance role must be
created through a separate governance amendment or stewardship transition.

## 3. Asset register

The maintainers should maintain a non-secret register of material assets and
administrators, including:

- GitHub organization ownership;
- domains and DNS;
- documentation hosting;
- Discord or other community services;
- package-registry namespaces;
- signing and release infrastructure;
- service accounts; and
- billing responsibility.

Passwords, recovery codes, private keys, personal addresses, and other secrets
must not appear in the public register.

## 4. Continuity

Where practical, critical services should:

- have at least two trusted administrators;
- use project-specific rather than shared personal accounts;
- have documented recovery procedures;
- use multi-factor authentication; and
- be transferable during a stewardship transition.

Asset transfer to Hexagon is governed by [STEWARDSHIP.md](STEWARDSHIP.md).

## 5. Policies not yet established

The project has not established:

- an annual budget or spending-approval threshold;
- reimbursement rules;
- a fiscal host or incorporated entity;
- ownership of the Briosa mark;
- a policy for holding cash donations;
- financial reporting intervals; or
- a corporate service-level or funding commitment.
