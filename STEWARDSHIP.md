# Stewardship and Institutional Transition

## 1. Current stewardship

The SpatialAnalyzer organization is currently independently administered by
the Project Founder and Lead Architect with the participation of the Hexagon
Primary and Backup Focals.

The Project Founder and Lead Architect currently controls or administers the
organization's principal assets and services, including GitHub organization
ownership, domains and DNS, community services, package registries, release
credentials, service accounts, and billing.

This arrangement is provisional. It does not, by itself, decide ownership of
trademarks, copyrights, individual contributions, or employer intellectual
property.

## 2. Hexagon's pre-authorized right to assume stewardship

Hexagon is pre-authorized by the project's founding governance to assume
formal stewardship of the project at any time. No additional project vote is
required to recognize that right.

The request must:

1. be made in writing by a representative authorized by Hexagon;
2. identify the person or team responsible for the transition; and
3. be authenticated to the reasonable satisfaction of the current
   maintainers.

The Hexagon Primary Focal and Project Founder and Lead Architect will
cooperate in good faith to implement the transition.

## 3. Transition plan

The parties will prepare a written transition plan covering, as applicable:

- the effective date and scope of stewardship;
- GitHub organization and repository administration;
- domains, DNS, websites, community services, and package registries;
- billing, donated services, and ongoing expenses;
- service accounts, credentials, signing keys, and recovery procedures;
- security reports and confidential project records;
- maintainer and release responsibilities;
- preservation of repository history and permanent founder attribution;
- the continuing role, if any, of each existing maintainer;
- public notice to users and contributors; and
- the license applied to future development.

The plan should be public except for credentials, security-sensitive details,
private personnel matters, and confidential legal or corporate information.

## 4. Licensing during a transition

Code and documentation already released under Apache-2.0 remain available
under their existing grants.

After assuming stewardship, Hexagon may set a different licensing policy for
future development. Any change is subject to applicable copyright ownership,
contributor grants, third-party licenses, contracts, and other legal rights.
This governance document does not itself transfer contributor copyrights or
authorize relicensing where additional permission is required.

## 5. No implied corporate transaction

A stewardship transition is an operational and governance change. It is not,
by itself, a merger, joint venture, transfer of employment, assignment of
copyright, trademark assignment, or assumption of liabilities. Any such
transaction requires its own valid agreement.

No other institutional stewardship path is established at this time.
