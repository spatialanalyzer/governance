# SpatialAnalyzer Organization Governance

This repository contains the governance documents for the
[SpatialAnalyzer GitHub organization](https://github.com/spatialanalyzer) and
its open-source projects.

The organization is initially focused on Briosa: an open-source gRPC bridge to
the SpatialAnalyzer SDK, together with language-specific thin clients,
documentation, and examples. The organization may host other open-source
projects related to the SpatialAnalyzer product.

## Governance status

These policies are **provisional**. They document the working agreement of the
Founding Maintainers as the organization begins operating; they are not a
complete corporate agreement between Hexagon and any other organization.
Unsettled topics are intentionally identified in [OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).

The project is currently independently administered and is being developed
with support from Hexagon. Hexagon is pre-authorized to assume formal
stewardship under the process in [STEWARDSHIP.md](STEWARDSHIP.md).

## Interpretation

These documents describe project policy, not legal advice and not authority to
act on behalf of Boeing, Hexagon, or another employer. If a project policy
conflicts with applicable law, a binding agreement, an employer requirement,
or the rights of a third party, the latter controls. The maintainers will
document the conflict and update project policy when appropriate.
