# Contributing

The SpatialAnalyzer organization welcomes contributions that advance the
mission in [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md). Project-specific build, test, and style
instructions belong in each code repository.

## License for contributions

Unless a contribution is explicitly submitted under another accepted
arrangement, contributions intentionally submitted for inclusion are provided
under the Apache License 2.0, consistent with the license of the receiving
project.

The project is likely to adopt the
[Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/), but
DCO sign-off is **not yet required**. Whether to require a DCO, CLA, or another
provenance mechanism remains an open governance question.

## Authority to contribute

By submitting a contribution, a contributor represents that they have the
right to submit it under the stated license. Contributors are responsible for
following their employers' policies.

Do not submit:

- confidential or proprietary code, documentation, data, or credentials;
- SpatialAnalyzer, SA SDK, or other closed-source Hexagon material;
- Boeing or other employer material without authorization;
- third-party code or generated artifacts without compatible redistribution
  rights; or
- test data containing customer, controlled, personal, or export-restricted
  information.

Generated COM interface definitions or similar artifacts may be accepted only
after the maintainers confirm that their inclusion and redistribution are
authorized. A prior informal assurance does not prevent maintainers from
requesting written confirmation for a particular artifact.

## Review

Founding Maintainers may independently commit and merge their own routine
work. Contributions from anyone else require review and approval by at least
one Founding Maintainer with suitable knowledge of the affected area.

Reviewers may request changes for correctness, compatibility, security,
licensing, maintainability, documentation, or fit with the project roadmap.
Acceptance is not guaranteed.

Breaking changes to the gRPC or Protocol Buffers API require the major-decision
process in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) and a documented migration path.

## Public record

Contributions, reviews, issues, and related account information form part of a
durable public project record. Security-sensitive information must instead
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Conduct

Participants are expected to communicate professionally and respectfully. A
formal code of conduct and enforcement process have not yet been adopted.
